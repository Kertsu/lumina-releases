# Lumina design

The release site uses a Dusk palette adapted to Lumina’s blue icon, its self-hosted Geist font, and the real beta captures.

## Surfaces and type

- Midnight for the home hero, navigation, and footer. Warm paper for the walkthrough, workflow, installer choices, and support content.
- [Tailwind theme tokens and base styles](app/globals.css) own the palette, type scale, spacing, container, and focus rings. Route components own their layout.
- Keep display type left-aligned and sentence case. Use captions to distinguish the operator workspace from the audience display.

## Controls

- Use a blue pill for the main download and the recommended installer. Use outlined pills or text links for secondary actions.
- Use ruled rows for installer choices and help. Keep keyboard focus visible and preserve the native video and disclosure controls.
- Use the supplied beta captures for product imagery. The hero shows the same passage on both screens.
- Center each support payment group around its QR code. Align the footer brand and grouped links to opposite edges on wide screens.
